## Completion Report — Document the `request_pull_request_reviewers` contract break

### What was done
- Documented that `ScmProvider.request_pull_request_reviewers()` returns `list[str]` instead of `None` since v1.2.0 (commit `eb55400`), with a migration guide for third-party subclasses.
- Contracted the existing `None` tolerance (read as "cannot verify") in the base docstring and covered it with tests.
- Made the log line honest when a legacy provider returns `None` (`(no read-back)` instead of `attached None`).
- Recorded the decision as ADR-0009 and added the **Read-back** term to the glossary.
